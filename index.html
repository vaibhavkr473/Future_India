Global Breakthrough

Sarvam AI: India Beats ChatGPT & Gemini on the World Stage

Bengaluru-based Sarvam AI has become India's first AI startup to outperform OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini on international benchmarks. Its Sarvam Vision OCR model scored 84.3% on the olmOCR-Bench — beating Gemini 3 Pro (80.2%), DeepSeek OCR v2 (78.8%), and ChatGPT (69.8%). On OmniDocBench v1.5, it hit 93.28%, surpassing Gemini's 91.6%.[reference:0]

Its flagship Sarvam 105B model — built entirely from scratch in India using IndiaAI Mission compute — scored 96.7 on AIME 2025 (maths) and 71.7 on LiveCodeBench v6 (coding), placing it among the world's top reasoning models. It outperformed Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash and DeepSeek R1 (600B parameters) on Indian language benchmarks.[reference:1]

Valuation: $1.5 billion (unicorn status) after raising $300 million Series B led by HCLTech and Nvidia.[reference:2] The company is now building a 1 trillion parameter model to challenge OpenAI and Anthropic directly.[reference:3]
